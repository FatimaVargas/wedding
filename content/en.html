Getting here

The journey to the mountains.

We chose Bad Gastein carefully, keeping an easy journey by train in mind. We hope you’ll enjoy the trip as much as the destination!

By train via Salzburg

There are direct trains from Salzburg Hauptbahnhof to Bad Gastein. If you’re staying overnight at a hotel or other accommodation in SalzburgerLand, you’ll receive a Guest Mobility Ticket, which covers this journey at no extra charge.

The ticket is valid throughout your stay, including arrival and departure days. Ask your accommodation to send it before you travel, through pre-check-in, so you can use it on your way to Bad Gastein.

Do you have a Deutschlandticket? Use eligible regional trains to Salzburg—from Munich directly, or from Bamberg with changes—then continue with your Guest Mobility Ticket. The Deutschlandticket does not cover ICE, IC or EC trains. However, the Guest Mobility Ticket includes ICE trains for the onward journey from Salzburg to Bad Gastein, within the province of Salzburg.

A little preparation

  • Hiking clothes and hiking shoes for exploring the mountains.
  • Comfortable shoes for walking around town.
  • Your dressy clothes for Saturday’s celebration.
  • Swimwear for the spa or pool area.
  • A jacket for spending time outside—it can get chilly at night.

🌦️ Check the weather forecast closer to the date. May can bring rain, so a raincoat or umbrella is always a good idea.

💰 A few money-saving tips

  • Travel for free: If you have a Deutschlandticket, use eligible regional trains to Salzburg, then your Guest Mobility Ticket for the onward train to Bad Gastein at no extra charge. Ask your accommodation to send the mobility ticket before arrival. See travel details ↗
  • Car sharing: If you decide to travel by car, consider sharing the journey with other guests making a similar trip. If you are a driver with a free seat or someone looking for a ride, let us know and we can try to match you.
  • Split your stay: book the earlier nights at the Bellevue Hotel or another hotel in town, then move to the Alm for Saturday night.
  • Check your corporate benefits: If your employer offers MONDI discounts through a corporate benefits programme, you may be able to use them when booking on the MONDI website. This does not apply to the Alm, where our rooms are already blocked at the base-season rates.
  • Travelling solo? If you’d like to share a double room, let us know! We can try to match you with another guest who would also be happy to share.
